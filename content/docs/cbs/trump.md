### 原文
<p>Trump takes part in online rallies as candidates make their final push.</p>
<p>What to know as Election Day approaches for the 2026 midterms:</p>
<p>With just three days to go until Election Day, Tuesday, Nov. 3, candidates in key Senate and House elections are making their closing arguments to midterm voters.</p>
<p>President Trump held a rally in Florida, continuing a weekslong blitz. He reversed course and said he will hold rallies in Kansas and Ohio this weekend.</p>
<p>The DOJ pushed to segregate some Alaska Senate ballots, pending a probe into how a man with nearly the same name as GOP Sen. Dan Sullivan ended up on the ballot. Democrat Mary Peltola denied involvement.</p>
<p>A California judge late Friday issued a temporary restraining order directing the federal government to stop demanding that California state and local election officials separate out ballots or not count certain votes from alleged noncitizens. The judge also ordered the DOJ to stop threatening officials with criminal prosecution if they do not comply.</p>
<p>Democratic Michigan Senate hopeful Abdul El-Sayed clashed with former primary rival Haley Stevens.</p>
<p>The latest CBS News polling shows Democrats with an edge in the House — but the Senate could hinge on toss-up elections in Maine, Ohio, Texas and more. CBS News now rates South Carolina as a GOP-leaning battleground.</p>
