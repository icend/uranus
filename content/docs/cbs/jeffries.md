### original
Jeffries says he's confident Democrats are "on the verge of taking back control"
House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries joined a rally in Virginia Beach on Saturday afternoon ahead of the battleground race for Virginia's 2nd Congressional district — a rematch between incumbent Republican Rep. Jen Kiggans and Democratic nominee Elaine Luria.

With Election Day just three days away, Jeffries told CBS News ahead of the rally that he's "very confident that House Democrats are on the verge of taking back control because the American people are fed up with MAGA extremism."

Asked what he'll view as a good night for Democrats on Tuesday, Jeffries replied, "the majority."

When it comes to whether he believes President Trump will accept the results of the election if Democrats win back one or both chambers, Jeffries told CBS News that Mr. Trump "will have no choice but to accept the results, because I believe that the American people are on the verge of delivering a decisive verdict."

Alex Johnson contributed to this report.
